scrolls horizontally at 1280 px once a window has ended with the long kill-switch label.**

label in any other way); open `/jenkins/batch-control/grants/` as admin and as the window holder.

on one line next to the nowrap ID/User/Scope/Actions/Names cells and "Request again". The Status cell of Grant
Requests ("window revoked (change control turned off)") already wraps. With the short labels ("Revoked by admin",
"Expired") the table fits (pass A).
Fix suggestion (ui-dev): drop `nowrap` on the How cell, or keep "Revoked" nowrap and let the parenthesis wrap.
