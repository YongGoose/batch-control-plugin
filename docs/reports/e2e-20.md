## Phase 1

- **Tracing** (`r6/lib.py`, which every CI driver uses): every browser context records a Playwright trace
  (screenshots, DOM snapshots), started after the login so the password is never in it, written on `done()`,
  `close()` and at interpreter exit (an `atexit` hook, so a driver that raised still writes it). The runner gives each
  step `traces/<nn>-<step>/` and deletes it when the step passed (`BC_TRACE=on` keeps all, `off` records none). The
  traces are inside `e2e/ci/out/<k>/`, which e2e.yml already uploads. Size: about 15 MB for the 18 contexts of round3.
  The traces earned their keep at once: a development run of round3 H failed with a 20 s timeout, and the trace showed
  that the role locator "More actions" resolved to the Jenkins header's own menu button (`title="More actions"`) before
  the app bar's overflow button; the step now uses `get_by_test_id`.
- **Login reuse**: the first context of an account logs in through the form and saves `storage_state` into a private
  temporary directory per shard (`BC_AUTH_DIR`, never in the artefacts, removed at the end); later contexts start from
  it and confirm the account with `/whoAmI/api/json`, and log in again when the session ended (restarts, logout,
  re-created account). Each `Session` is still its own context. `Session(user, fresh=True)` and `BC_LOGIN_REUSE=0`
  opt out. Checked before relying on it: no CI driver uses the page a login leaves behind (each first navigates).
- **Fonts**: e2e.yml installs `fonts-liberation` and copies `e2e/ci/fonts.conf` to `~/.config/fontconfig/`, mapping
  `system-ui`, `-apple-system` and `sans-serif` to Liberation Sans (Arial metrics), and prints `fc-match` to the log.
  round3 compares `scrollWidth` with `clientWidth` (not the literal 1280) plus a documented 2 px tolerance
  (`SCROLL_TOLERANCE_PX`) for sub-pixel rounding; a real overflow still fails. In the Playwright Linux image
  (`mcr.microsoft.com/playwright/python:v1.63.0-noble`) without the config, `system-ui` resolved to WenQuanYi Zen Hei;
  the runner's default is DejaVu Sans. The fix is not verified on GitHub yet (this branch is not pushed).

## Phase 2 (condition waits, chosen from the baseline)

The baseline's time was in jobui (8 steps, 58 min), the crawls (15 steps, 50 min), the seed (5 x 104 s) and actions;
each was paced by fixed waits after clicks. New helpers in `r6/lib.py`: `react()` (a click ends its wait on a dialog,
a navigation or a new menu; when none happens the click is observed for the same time as before and classified as
before), `dialog_ready()` (core opens a form dialog as an empty shell with only its Close button and fills it later;
wait for a form/field or for two buttons), `opened()`, `gone()`, `until()`, `submit_and_wait()` (navigation, the form
replaced by the server's re-render, or closed) and `poll()`.

## Phase 3 (runner)

- `e2e/ci/test_shard.py` + `conftest.py`: one pytest test per step (`--bc-shard k/N --bc-out DIR`), each running the
  existing driver through `shard.py`'s new `Runner` class, so verdicts, logs, traces and `summary.json/md` are the same
  as `shard.py run` (kept: `BC_RUNNER=legacy`). JUnit XML (`junit.xml`: seconds, log and traces per testcase); steps
  after a failed setup step are skipped as BLOCKED. `ci/junit_summary.py` puts the JUnit counts and failed steps at
  the top of the job summary without a third-party action (fork PRs keep working with the read-only token).
- Retries only for `shard.FLAKY` (`@pytest.mark.flaky(reruns=1)`, pytest-rerunfailures; `shard.py run` does the same).
  The set is empty: no evidence of an intermittent step. Checked with a fake driver that fails once: retried, passed,
  the failed attempt's log and its two traces kept, listed under "retried"; `BC_FLAKY=round3:round3 pytest
  --collect-only -m flaky` selects exactly that step.
- pytest-playwright where it fits: the content-check self-test (`ci/test_selftest_content.py`, its `page` fixture,
  `--tracing retain-on-failure` in CI). The shard drivers keep their own browser (subprocesses).
- Node drivers: `browser/` (167 files), `extra/` (17), `fresh/` (36) moved to `e2e/legacy/` after checking that
  `ci/shard.py` runs only Python drivers and nothing outside those directories imports or calls them. `scripts/*.sh`
  and the r12/r13/r14 shell runners stay (environment lifecycle, documented local entry points).
- e2e.yml validated with actionlint 1.7.12 + shellcheck 0.11.0 (clean). `run.sh` keeps one pre-existing SC2155
  warning.
